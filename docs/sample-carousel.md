## Slide 1: The Hook (Curiosity & Reframing)

- **Header Tag:** `THE HARD TRUTH`
- **Headline:** Stop buying top-of-funnel traffic until your Net Dollar Retention hits 110%.
- **Body:** Most early SaaS startups don't die from lack of leads. They die from an invisible leaky bucket that venture capital only accelerates.
- **Art Direction & Visual Note:** High-contrast editorial serif, generous whitespace, author stamp in top header, oversized quotation glyph.
- **Counter:** `01 / 07`

## Slide 2: The Problem (The Hidden Decay)

- **Header Tag:** `THE PARADOX`
- **Headline:** The Acquisition Trap: Why \$100k in paid ads is masking product decay.
- **Body:** When monthly churn is 4%, you need to replace nearly 40% of your entire customer base every year just to stay flat. You aren't building an enterprise; you're running on a treadmill.
- **Art Direction & Visual Note:** Split-metric diagram comparing compound drain of churn against compounding expansion.
- **Counter:** `02 / 07`

---

## Slide 3: The Framework (The Underlying Law)

- **Header Tag:** `THE METRIC`
- **Headline:** The Golden Ratio: NDR > 115% means negative revenue churn.
- **Body:** Negative churn means expansion within existing accounts outpaces all cancellations and downgrades. Even if you turn off marketing tomorrow, your ARR compounds by 15% automatically.
- **Art Direction & Visual Note:** Horizontal metric visual comparing linear acquisition curves vs. exponential cohort expansion curves.
- **Counter:** `03 / 07`

## Slide 4: Action Step 1 (Onboarding Velocity)

- **Header Tag:** `STEP 01`
- **Headline:** Instrument feature adoption velocity within the first 14 days.
- **Body:** Customers don't churn at month 12. They churn emotionally on day 4 when their initial integration fails or their first team member is left uninvited.
- **Art Direction & Visual Note:** Tactical checklist card with 14-day milestone indicators and telemetry checkmarks.
- **Counter:** `04 / 07`

## Slide 5: Action Step 2 (Value-Based Pricing)

- **Header Tag:** `STEP 02`
- **Headline:** Price on value-metric scaling, not arbitrary seat tiers.
- **Body:** Align your billing with what your customer celebrates: API calls processed, documents parsed, or revenue transacted. As they win, your contract expands automatically.
- **Art Direction & Visual Note:** Contrast table between static per-seat tax vs. shared outcome expansion.
- **Counter:** `05 / 07`

## Slide 6: The Summary (Manifesto)

- **Header Tag:** `SUMMARY CHEAT-SHEET`
- **Headline:** The Retention Engine in Three Rules:
- **Body:**
  1. Never scale paid channels without a 90-day cohort plateau.
  2. Tie annual pricing directly to their business value metric.
  3. Make onboarding speed your #1 engineering KPI.
